How Strong Are Your SWOT Skills?

Separating strengths and weaknesses from opportunities and threats is one of the most important, and often most challenging, aspects of developing a SWOT analysis.

In this exercise, you'll test your ability to classify real-world data points and receive immediate feedback on your decisions.

The exercise is ungraded, so don't worry about making mistakes. In fact, that's part of the learning process. Even experienced strategists sometimes disagree on how to classify a data point.

Complete the challenge as many times as you'd like and see whether you can improve your score with each attempt.

Remember

  • • Strengths and Weaknesses are internal factors.
  • • Opportunities and Threats are external factors.
